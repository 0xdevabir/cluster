1. **Final electrical design:** M1 uses **2x 30 A 3-phase circuits per rack (Option B)** —
   `r01-circuit-a` feeding `r01-pdu-a`, `r01-circuit-b` feeding `r01-pdu-b`
   (`inventory/power/circuits.yaml`). Both currently sit at ~23% of their 80%-derated limit
   (5.56 A of a 24 A derated ceiling) — large headroom by design, since the circuit size was
   chosen against the M4 target of 12 compute-gpu nodes/rack (49% of derated at that shape),
   not the current 8-node M1 population. **Status: designed, not quoted or installed.**
   Feeders should be pulled sized for the M4 400 A service even though only these two 30 A
   circuits are needed now — see `docs/facility/load-study.md` staged buildout table.

2. **Cooling design:** Mini-split/ductless, ≥1.5 t, no containment at M1 (single pilot
   rack). **N+1 redundancy becomes mandatory the moment M2 provisioning starts** — this is
   an open gap, not yet contracted; do not grow past the current 8-node population without a
   second cooling unit in place. Full detail and the M4 in-row-cooler recommendation in
   `docs/facility/cooling-plan.md`. **Status: design only, no equipment purchased.**

3. **PDU model and API type:** Raritan PX3 series (switched+metered, 3-phase 208V/30A,
   SNMPv3 + REST/JSON API) is the named primary candidate; Vertiv Geist rPDU and APC Rack
   PDU 2G are acceptable alternates meeting the same criteria. **Exact SKU is not committed
   — get it from the quote (Task 7/R-14) before Phase 08 integrates against a specific API.**
   Phase 08 should target the general capability contract in
   `docs/facility/power-control-design.md` (per-outlet switch/meter, SNMPv3-or-REST) rather
   than assuming a specific vendor SDK until the SKU is confirmed.

4. **PDU management IPs and credential locations:** `r01-pdu-a` = `10.100.1.11`,

9. **`inventory/racks.yaml` intentionally covers only `r01`.** `r02`-`r08` are a load-study
   projection, not inventory records — do not let a future phase's tooling assume they exist
   (see `deviations.md` D2). When M2 hardware is real, add rack records the same way Phase
   01 added node records: `status: planned` first, `active` once physically installed.

10. **Single-PSU nodes are not dual-corded.** `inventory/power/pdu-map.yaml` alternates each
    node's `primary` feed between PDU-A/B and reserves a same-numbered `failover` outlet on
    the other PDU for a manual cord-swap — it does not provide automatic sub-second
    failover. Any future phase (Phase 32's scheduler, Phase 45's alerting) that assumes a PDU
    outage is transparently absorbed should account for this — see
    `docs/facility/power-control-design.md` "Single-PSU load balancing" and
    `deviations.md` D4.

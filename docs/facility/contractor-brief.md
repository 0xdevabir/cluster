`power-control-design.md`, `acoustic-and-safety.md` (all in this directory).

## 1. Electrical scope

- **Service:** 208 V 3-phase. M1 immediate need: **2x 30 A 3-phase circuits** (Option B —
  A/B redundant feeds to rack r01). Design the feeder/conduit run for the **M4 target of
  400 A 3-phase** even though only the M1 circuits are commissioned now — pulling conduit
  once oversized is far cheaper than pulling it twice (see `load-study.md` staged buildout).
- **Branch circuits:** 2x 30 A 3-phase per rack, one rack at M1. Receptacle: IEC 60309
  3P+N+E, 30A (confirm against the PDU model chosen in `power-control-design.md` once
  quoted).
- **Panel location, conduit runs, EPO:** **not yet specified — this is exactly what we need
  your site survey for.** We do not know the existing service voltage/phase, main breaker
  rating, spare panel capacity, or room type (see `evidence/phase-02/electrician-signoff.md`).
- **Staged buildout schedule:** M1 (now, 1 rack) -> M2 (~16.2 kW, 2 racks) -> M3 (~32.4 kW,
  4 racks) -> M4 (68.1 kW, 8 racks). Full table in `load-study.md`.

## 3. Cooling scope

| Milestone | Heat load | Tons required (1.15x IT load) | CFM (ΔT 20°F) | Containment | Redundancy |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| M1 | 13,667 BTU/h | 1.31 t | 633 CFM | None (pilot) | N |
| M4 | 232,197 BTU/h | 22.2 t | 10,750 CFM | Full hot/cold aisle | N+1 (mandatory from M2) |

- ΔT target: ≤ 12 °C (22 °F) intake-to-exhaust.
- Intake temperature: 18-24 °C (consumer GPUs throttle above this despite ASHRAE A2 allowing
  up to 35 °C).
- Humidity: 40-60% RH.
- Sensors/interlocks: 2x intake + 2x exhaust probes per rack, 1x room temp/humidity per zone,
  thermal shutdown interlock above ~30 °C sustained (triggers graceful cluster shutdown via
  the power-control design). Full detail in `cooling-plan.md`.

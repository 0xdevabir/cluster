**A5 — cooling capacity ≥ 1.15 x IT load, with N+1 from M2:**

- M1: required capacity = 1.15 x 1.14 t = **1.31 t**. A single 1.5 t mini-split/ductless
  unit covers this with margin. N+1 is **not required at M1** per Task 2's own rule
  ("Redundancy: N+1 on cooling from M2 onward") — a single-unit failure at M1's 4 kW load
  gives room temperature rise of roughly 1 °C per several minutes in a room this small,
  survivable long enough for WoL/PDU-based graceful shutdown (Task 5 escalation ladder).
- M2 onward: **N+1 is mandatory.** This is flagged as an open facility requirement, not yet
  contracted — see `docs/facility/contractor-brief.md` and `evidence/phase-02/handoff.md`
  item 8 ("anything the facility cannot support"). Do not grow past M1 node count without a
  second cooling unit in place.
- M4: required capacity = 1.15 x 19.3 t = **22.2 t**. An in-row cooler line sized to 24-30 t
  aggregate (N+1 across the row) meets this.

## Mandatory design elements (Task 2, all milestones)

1. **Hot/cold aisle separation.** `inventory/racks.yaml` records `coldAisleTempC` per rack;
   every node's intake faces the aisle documented in `docs/facility/rack-layout.md`.
2. **Containment.** M1: none (single rack, pilot). M2+: curtains minimum, doors+roof ideal —
   required before N+1 redundancy has any effect, since without containment the two cooling
   units fight the same recirculating air instead of sharing a sealed cold aisle.
3. **Blanking panels in every empty U.** r01 currently has U9-U36 open (8 of 42U populated,
   see `inventory/racks.yaml` notes) — **blanking panels for U9-U36 are required before
   commissioning**, tracked as a facility punch-list item, not optional cosmetic work.
4. **Brush grommets on every cable cutout** (PDU cabling, NIC DAC runs).
5. **ΔT target ≤ 12 °C (22 °F)** between cold-aisle intake and hot-aisle exhaust — measured at
   the node, not the room thermostat (see Troubleshooting in `phases/PHASE-02.md`).
6. **Intake temperature 18-24 °C** — recorded per rack in `inventory/racks.yaml`
   `coldAisleTempC`.
7. **Humidity 40-60% RH.**
8. **Redundancy: N+1 from M2.** See above — currently an open gap for M2, not yet contracted.
9. **Thermal shutdown interlock.** A room-temperature sensor above threshold (design target:
   30 °C sustained) triggers `talosctl shutdown` across the fleet via the power-control
   escalation ladder (`docs/facility/power-control-design.md`). Wiring is Phase 32's job;
   this phase only specifies the requirement and the trigger threshold.

## Instrumentation (specified now, wired by Phase 45)

- 2x intake + 2x exhaust temperature probes per rack (r01: 4 probes minimum)
- 1x room temperature + humidity sensor for zone-a
- Per-outlet power metering on both r01-pdu-a and r01-pdu-b (native to the chosen PDU model,
  see `power-control-design.md`)
- Differential pressure sensor across containment — **deferred until containment exists (M2)**
- Water-leak detection — not applicable at M1/M2 (no chilled water); required if/when a
  rear-door heat exchanger or chilled-water CRAH is chosen for M3/M4
